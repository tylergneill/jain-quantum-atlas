### booktext sample (22 items, seeded random, Sanskrit-weighted)

16 rows with a `file_size` all returned full text with page anchors equal to
`num_pages` (±1); 6 rows with blank `file_size` returned empty text. Text
density 847–4,182 characters per page, mean 1,914; Devanāgarī share of
characters 0.55 on average across the Sanskrit-tagged items (the rest is
Latin, Gujarati, digits, punctuation and OCR noise). A rough plaintext volume
for any-Sanskrit rows is therefore ~3.3 G characters, of which perhaps
~1.8 G Devanāgarī — an order-of-magnitude estimate until measured.

## OCR docx bytes as a proxy for plaintext (`calibrate_docx.py`)

15 sampled items had both an API `OCR docx` size and a measured Quantum text.
Above ~100 KB of docx: 1.5–2.1 characters per docx byte (median 1.67, pooled
1.84); ~1.2 Devanāgarī characters per byte on Sanskrit-tagged items; small
items 0.4–0.5 because of ~40–50 KB fixed docx overhead. Applied to the API
totals: any-Sanskrit 1.47 GB docx → ~2.6 G characters (~1.8 G Devanāgarī);
Sanskrit-only 0.63 GB → ~1.1 G characters (~0.75 G Devanāgarī). Both agree
with the independent page-count estimate above.

**Coverage mismatch.** Three sampled Quantum rows in `master_folder` `agam`
(035040, 006332, 021005) and one in `jaina_edu` (000008) are in none of the
five API item endpoints. Quantum has 1,483 `agam` rows and 180 `jaina_edu`
rows; the API has 39,588 items to Quantum's 33,048. The two catalogues overlap
heavily but neither contains the other.
