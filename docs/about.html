Jain Quantum Atlas About

About this Atlas

A browsable index of the Jain eLibrary, an online library of Jain literature run by JAINA's education arm, as served by its two public faces: jainqq.org ("Jain Quantum"), a full-text search engine that publishes each item's OCR text, and jainelibrary.org, whose open API carries the richer metadata. This Atlas hosts no text; every item links back to Quantum's viewer.

What is and is not counted

This Atlas publishes the Sanskrit tier of the library and nothing else: items whose catalogued language is exactly Sanskrit and that are books. "Books" here includes two of the library's storage folders: "manuscripts", which holds block prints and horizontal-format printed books (marked horizontal), and Quantum's Āgama folder of sutra-level editions. That is what Sāgarasaṅgama reads from it. The rest of the library (Gujarati and Hindi books, articles, magazines, audio, and editions that pair Sanskrit with a translation) is counted in the muted rows above and otherwise left out. The two catalogues are joined on their shared six-digit serial. An item has text when Quantum serves a booktext for it; every such item has been fetched and measured, so the byte figures are complete.

Browsing

The default axis is the author: the classical author where the catalogue names one (sutra_author), otherwise the author or editor the catalogue gives, marked ed. on the row; 96% of the Sanskrit items have one. The second axis is the Āgama canon: class (Aṅga, Upāṅga, Chedasūtra, Mūlasūtra, Cūlikāsūtra, Prakīrṇaka) and canonical text, assigned from the library's own agam_… tags and nothing else, with the classes in their traditional order. of the Sanskrit items carry such a tag; the rest sit under Other works. Horizontal-format prints and script are facts on the row, not shelves.

Where Quantum stops

Quantum's catalog and text index end in 2022, and the library's OCR pipeline appears to have stopped with it. Sanskrit items accessioned since are listed here from the API alone, flagged API only, with no public text and no size. Because the cataloguing convention changed at the same time (recent Sanskrit editions are tagged "Sanskrit, Hindi"), few of them fall in the Sanskrit tier at all.

Growth

Every API record carries the day it went online, so the growth series is measured from the library's own stamps: months, to ; items known only to Quantum carry no date. The text series flattens after 2022 for the reason above.

Caveats

  • The text is OCR, with its noise still in. The furniture rules remove the digitising libraries' stamps and page numbers and nothing else.
  • "Sanskrit" per the catalogue includes many books with Hindi apparatus in the same script, and the language field's convention changed around 2023, so any Sanskrit is the stable grouping.
  • Classification on the source is mostly by script and format; subject tokens exist on some records but are not surfaced on either site.
